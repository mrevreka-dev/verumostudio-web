Moko & Pim

Songs and tiny stories for little ones

Moko and his four friends sing, dance and get through a toddler's day together: bath time, brushing teeth, a small fall, bedtime. Every song has a simple move a child can copy, and every word is on screen as it is sung.

YouTubeInstagram

Meet the friends

  • Moko

    Cheerful, sleepy, loves snacks and hugs. Tries, fails, tries again.

    Moko-moko!

  • Pim

    Moko's best friend. A little robot who loves counting; its screen shows how it feels.

    Beep-boop!

  • Lulu

    A bouncy, brave bunny who loves dancing and jumping in puddles.

    Hop-hop-hooray!

  • Bobo

    Gentle and a bit shy. Gets scared, then finds out he is brave.

    Uh-oh… it's okay!

  • Gogo

    The funny one, with the biggest laugh. Loves water, singing and food.

    Ho-ho-hoo!
