Moko & Pim

Original animated comedy

Moko is a lazy creature with a secretly brilliant talent for schemes. Pim is a tiny, over-organized robot who wants to control everything. They share an apartment. Home, work, tech, shopping, holidays: nothing stays under control for long.

What you'll find

  • Shorts of 20–40 seconds, one joke each
  • A monthly compilation
  • Almost no dialogue, so every joke works in any language

Made for viewers aged 16 to 28. This is not a kids' channel. Human-directed, AI-assisted production; Moko and Pim are original characters owned by Verumo Studio.
