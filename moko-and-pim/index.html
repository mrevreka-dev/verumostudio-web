Moko & Pim

Original animated comedy

Two flatmates, one apartment, and nothing stays under control for long. Short comedy with almost no dialogue, so every joke works in any language.

YouTubeInstagram

Moko

A lazy, round, orange creature with a secretly brilliant talent for schemes. Will do anything to avoid getting up, including inventing something extraordinary.

Pim

A tiny, over-organised robot who wants to control everything: the snacks, the chores, the step count. Usually standing on Moko's head.

The first episodes

Shorts of 20 to 40 seconds, one joke each, plus a monthly compilation. Home, work, tech, shopping, holidays.

  • Pim padlocked the snacks. Moko didn't get up.
  • The smart home obeys beeps. Moko learned to boop.
  • The chore wheel gave him everything. He built a throne.
  • He hit 10,000 steps without taking a single one.

Made for viewers aged 16 to 28; this is not a kids' channel. Human-directed, AI-assisted production. Moko and Pim are original characters owned by Verumo Studio.
